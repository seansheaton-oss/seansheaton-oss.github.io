Drawnity Privacy Policy

Developer: Toyloom Games
Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Drawnity - Photo to Sketch (Android package com.drawwifypro.app) handles information. For privacy questions, contact seansheaton@gmail.com.

Photos and on-device processing

Drawnity accesses photos that you choose through Android's photo or document picker, or that you capture using a camera app. Photo effects run locally on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers to apply effects. Drawnity does not require you to create an account.

Local files and preferences

The app stores editing state, temporary working images, settings, redeemed complimentary access codes and purchase-entitlement information on your device. When you choose to save an artwork, Drawnity writes the exported image to your device's picture library, normally Pictures/Drawnity. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-private data; exported pictures may remain in your photo library until you delete them.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you choose the Android share action and select a destination. The chosen app or service receives the image you share and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your photos or personal information.

Google Play purchases

Optional PRO purchases and restoration are handled by Google Play. Drawnity receives purchase information, including purchase status and signed purchase receipts, to validate and restore access to paid features. A signed receipt may be stored in encrypted form on your device, using Android Keystore, so that previously verified access can work offline. Drawnity does not receive your full payment-card details. Google processes payment and related service data under its privacy policy. Photos are not uploaded for purchase verification.

Advertising and analytics

The current Drawnity app contains no advertising or developer-operated analytics SDK. This policy does not replace the privacy notices of Android, Google Play, or services you separately choose to use.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Complimentary PRO access

If Toyloom Games provides an access code, you can redeem it in Settings. The code is verified on your device and stored locally to remember access. It is not uploaded to our servers. You can remove complimentary access in Settings or clear app storage.

Security and your choices

You control which images you select, whether to save or share them, and whether to make a purchase. Use Android settings to manage app storage and available permissions. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Drawnity is a general-purpose photo editor and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or photo-upload service. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support message, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Contact Toyloom Games at seansheaton@gmail.com with questions or requests.
