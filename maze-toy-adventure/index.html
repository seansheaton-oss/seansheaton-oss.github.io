Toyloom Games

Maze Toy Adventure

Little steps. Big discoveries.

In development · Google Play coming soon

A little red friend. A world of toy mazes.

Help your smiling red block reach the green goal, one small step at a time. Explore 50 distinct maze layouts across five colorful chapters, with bright blue walls and playful toy scenery. From level 26, collect a yellow key to open a gate and discover a new path.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Tap an adjacent cell, swipe, or use the large direction buttons. There is no timer. Unlimited hints and Undo help you try a different path, and generous star goals leave room to explore.

A COMPLETE OFFLINE ADVENTURE
One upfront purchase includes all 50 puzzles. No ads, in-app purchases or account. Your progress and preferences are saved locally. Optional email feedback and store ratings open external apps and may need internet access.

MADE FOR SHARED DISCOVERIES
Play independently or solve a puzzle together. Choose English or Simplified Chinese, turn gentle sounds on or off, and reduce animation when preferred. Celebrate each goal with your red friend, earn chapter trophies, and return to find all 150 stars.

For grown-ups: feedback and external store links use a parent check. No reading speed or fast reactions are required to move through a maze.
